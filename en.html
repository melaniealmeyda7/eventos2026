Grand Prize Winner Desafío Kunan 2026

RONIN Amazon Footwear & Biomaterials

Peru's first turf soccer shoe crafted with a wild Amazonian rubber sole
“A clear mission: making the standing forest worth more than cut down.”

Wild Harvesting & Shiringuero Families

Born from over a decade of collaboration alongside shiringuero families of the Pichis River (Puerto Bermúdez, Pasco), in the buffer zone of the El Sira Communal Reserve.

Rubber is harvested without felling a single tree: shiringueros make a delicate cut on the bark, latex flows naturally, and the tree remains alive and productive for decades.

Environmental & Economic Impact

Because household income depends on keeping the forest standing, safeguarding the ecosystem is the most profitable economic decision.

Wild Amazonian rubber emits up to six times less CO₂e per kilogram than traditional synthetic rubber, actively conserving hectares of pristine Amazon rainforest.

Ronin Lab: Next-Gen Biomaterials

At Ronin Lab, researchers develop wild shiringa latex biotextiles and blend indigenous Amazonian fibers such as aguaje, chambira palm, and banana stem into accessories and low-carbon circular materials.

Every pair and product purchased increases direct demand for raw materials from the standing forest, generating fair and sustainable income for guardian families.

Professional Bio & Background

María Gracia Agurto is CEO of Golden Circle, an active angel investor, and specialist in venture capital and investment readiness. She previously co-founded a MedTech startup in Israel and has worked with premier venture capital funds across the United States, Latin America, and Israel, including OurCrowd, Magma Partners, and Ganas Ventures.

She has evaluated hundreds of startups across international markets and is a Fellow of the U.S. Department of State. She holds an MBA in Innovation and Startups from The Hebrew University of Jerusalem, completed Global Competitiveness Leadership at Georgetown University, and is an Industrial & Commercial Engineer from ESAN.

About Golden Circle

Golden Circle is a strategic advisory firm based in Miami that works with startups and accelerators across the United States, Canada, and Latin America to prepare founders for real conversations with institutional investors.

Their methodology combines investment readiness, investor narratives, pitch deck reviews, and fundraising strategy. Since 2024, they have advised over 80 startups that have collectively raised over $7M in capital.
